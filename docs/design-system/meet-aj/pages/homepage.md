# Homepage override

- Hero name is white on the photograph (scrim on the left). Never leak that fill into Article H1.
- Hero CTAs use Article button geometry, not glass `btn-modern`.
- Get to Know Me: two-column editorial; H2 bar; no tiny stat cards in the intro.
- Skills: domain lists, no progress bars, no green “skill” meaning.
- Resume: timeline, not cards.
- Article filters: kicker pills, 44×44px minimum.
- Search toolbar: DNA inputs + primary search button.
- Testimonials: one quote surface (allowed card).
- Contact: one form surface; method list is typography, not glass tiles.
