# Article pages override

Master applies to chrome only.

- Do **not** restyle article HTML, code blocks, TOC, or topic themes (`theme-linux` green, `theme-mikrotik` orange, `theme-vmware` indigo).
- Breadcrumbs, tags, share, related: editorial lists, not a card grid.
- Share links may inherit the article topic color; that is DNA, not a site leak.
- Library search results are a numbered/typographic list, not Isotope cards.
